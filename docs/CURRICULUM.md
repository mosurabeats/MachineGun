# LINEAGE curriculum design & learning resources

## Mission

LINEAGE is a complete tattoo apprenticeship study program, not a flash-painting course. It covers:

* machine operation
* skin preparation and stencils
* needle selection
* lining, shading and color
* working with clients
* sanitation and healing

It combines four modes of learning:

1. **Independent study**: chapter-length lessons, diagrams, interactive tools and a question bank
2. **Synthetic-skin practice**: drill progressions and assignments that need photo or video evidence
3. **Feedback**: AI feedback on what is visible in uploads, plus milestones your mentor observes in person
4. **Supervised client tattooing**: only after your mentor signs the prerequisite milestones

## Progression

```
Study + Workshop + Synthetic practice ──► ★ mentor milestones (observed in person)
                                           ├─ bloodborne pathogens training
                                           ├─ barrier & cross-contamination practice
                                           ├─ full procedure on synthetic skin
                                           ├─ lining (synthetic)
                                           ├─ shading & packing (synthetic)
                                           └─ mentor readiness decision
                                        ──► supervised client session log unlocks
                                        ──► healing reviews ──► human-skin milestones
                                        ──► portfolio ──► mentor judges you ready to work independently
```

## Allocation

The default weighting is:

| Area | Share |
|---|---|
| Design | 30% |
| Machine and synthetic practice | 25% |
| Safety, anatomy and procedure | 20% |
| History and theory | 15% |
| Assessment and portfolio | 10% |

You can adjust it on The Path. It is always normalised to 100%, and a change made in Mentor Mode is labelled as set by your mentor.
